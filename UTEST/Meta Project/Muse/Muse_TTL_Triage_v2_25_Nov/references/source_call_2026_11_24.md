# Source material for v2

This skill version was built from two records of the **same** TTL onboarding
call, held 2026-11-24 (~90 minutes), presented by a TTL (Bravin Ian Ruto)
roughly 3 weeks into the Muse project, with live Q&A from other TTLs
(Victor Gaviria, Bhavana Machchar, Muralidhar Baini) and TE Lead
Ivonne Alition / Jose Ribeiro referenced as Slack contacts:

1. A private YouTube recording of the full screen-share, auto-dubbed to
   Vietnamese (the transcript SKILL.md and the other references draw their
   direct detail from).
2. An English Gemini meeting-notes auto-summary of the same call, used to
   cross-check names, terminology and the parts of the Vietnamese transcript
   that were only lightly summarized here.

## Gemini meeting-notes summary (verbatim)

> Board assignments and sheet workflows overviewed, with bonus structures
> and slotting procedures detailed via operational demonstrations.
>
> **Monday board and cycle assignments** — Monday board serves as the
> central hub for accessing test platforms and tester sheets. Primary focus
> should be given to assigned cycles while maintaining a single Slack
> thread per cycle daily.
>
> **External sheet validation and bonuses** — Results sync automatically to
> external sheets where TTLs attach video links and validate steps. Bonuses
> offer $10 for cases completed within 6 hours and $20 for weekend priority
> cycles.
>
> **Tester slotting and progress tracking** — Management tool scripts help
> isolate correct slot numbers to prevent confusion when multiple testers
> work on shared sheets. Checking chat frequently helps track progress and
> manage heavy workloads during cycles.

## Points from the Gemini notes not otherwise detailed in SKILL.md

- The **"local connectors UK → different tab name"** mismatch was raised by
  Victor Gaviria specifically; the platform-side label cited was
  "P[O] UK regional retail" (transcribed slightly differently between the
  two sources — "PO UK regional retail" in the Vietnamese transcript).
- The **EasyJet / Android / test cases 122-123** slotting example involved
  a tester named **Oming** who hadn't put their name in the results column
  — the TTL split slots up to 121, then split 122-123 separately so Oming
  could find the right column. This is the second worked slotting example
  in the call (the first being JD Sports / test case 4), both describing
  the same Split → Split-a-slot-in-row mechanic documented in
  `tester_slotting.md`.
- Reference videos for slotting specifically are said to already exist on
  the team's Slack channel — worth searching for before re-asking the
  question there.

## Known transcription caveat

The Vietnamese auto-dub consistently renders the project name as **"News"**
— this is read as **Muse** throughout SKILL.md and the other references in
this folder, not a separate/different project.
